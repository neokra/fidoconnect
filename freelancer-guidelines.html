Professional Standards

Freelancer Guidelines

FidoConnect is designed for dedicated professionals who want access to suitable small project opportunities. Please review our standards for quality, integrity, and conduct.

👤 1. Profile & Skills Accuracy

Your skill profile is how FidoConnect matches you with suitable client briefs. Maintaining accurate data is mandatory:

  • Truthful Information: Provide only genuine information regarding your experience, skills, and past work.
  • Select Real Capabilities: Only check service categories and tools you actively know and have executed before.
  • No Exaggerations: Do not claim proficiencies or experience levels you do not possess.
  • Keep Profiles Updated: Update your bio, categories, and contact details as your skillset evolves.

📋 2. Project Applications & Proposals

Applications are reviewed directly by agency coordinators. To maintain efficiency and fairness:

  • Skill-Matched Applications Only: Apply only for projects that match your verified skill categories.
  • Respect Plan Limits: Adhere to the concurrent application limit permitted by your active membership tier. Do not attempt to bypass plan limits.
  • Read Before Applying: Review client requirements, deliverables, budget, and deadlines thoroughly before submitting a proposal.
  • No Generic Spam: Write concise, tailored proposal notes explaining how you plan to complete the specific task. Avoid repetitive copy-paste text.

⚡ 3. Work Quality & Deadlines

When you are assigned to a project, the client and agency rely on your professional execution:

  • Professional Standards: Complete all work to a clean, production-ready standard without bugs, broken layouts, or formatting errors.
  • Follow Specifications: Adhere strictly to the agreed brief, required features, and deliverables.
  • Meet Agreed Deadlines: Submit work on or before the committed timeframe.
  • Proactive Communication: If a technical issue, blocker, or unexpected delay occurs, notify the coordinator or client immediately.
  • Zero Disappearance Tolerance: Never abandon an accepted project or stop responding after assignment. Disappearing results in permanent account suspension.

🛡️ 4. Originality & Licensing

Respect for intellectual property is essential to building trust:

  • No Plagiarism or Stolen Work: Never submit copied, stolen, or pirated code, designs, or text.
  • Authentic Portfolio: Never present work created by others as your own creation.
  • Respect Licenses: Ensure any stock assets, open-source libraries, or templates used comply with commercial licensing terms.

🤝 5. Professional Conduct & Ethics

  • No Harassment or Abuse: Maintain courteous, respectful communication at all times.
  • No Fraudulent Activity: Any attempt to submit fake UTR reference numbers, impersonate others, or falsify credentials will result in immediate deactivation.
  • System Integrity: Do not attempt to exploit, scrape, or bypass FidoConnect's platform systems.

⭐ 6. Membership & Opportunity Reality

FidoConnect operates as a curated matching bridge:

Important Reality Check: An active membership unlocks the ability to view details and submit proposals for matching project opportunities according to your plan tier. Membership does NOT guarantee project assignment, continuous work, or fixed earnings. Project volume depends on incoming client requirements.

Membership, application, and project statuses can adjust dynamically based on platform rules, project completions, and guideline adherence.
