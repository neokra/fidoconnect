Curated Project Coordination

How FidoConnect Works

"Small projects. Selected freelancers. A simpler way to get work done."

FidoConnect is not an open, noisy bidding marketplace where hundreds of random proposals flood every job. We operate a curated, invite-only coordination model that pairs clear project requirements with verified, skill-matched professionals.

🔒

Invite-Only Network

Freelancers join exclusively via verified invitation codes. No fake accounts, spam bids, or unvetted participants.

🎯

Precision Skill-Matching

Opportunities are unlocked only for specialists verified in the specific service category required for that project.

🤝

Dedicated Coordination

Our agency operations team reviews briefs, vets proposals, assists milestones, and ensures smooth delivery.

👤

The Client Process

From task brief to completed delivery in 8 straightforward steps.

1

Small Project Requirement

You need a small digital task completed (e.g. portfolio site, data entry, landing page, graphic design).

2

Submit Project Brief

Post your project details, required deliverables, budget range, and timeline on our platform.

3

Agency Review

FidoConnect operations reviews your brief for scope clarity, feasibility, and technical parameters.

4

Opportunity Prepared

The opportunity is structured and categorized for eligible specialists in our network.

5

Skill Matching & Visibility

Only verified freelancers matching your exact skill category can view and apply to the opportunity.

6

Specialist Selected & Assigned

FidoConnect reviews proposals and assigns the most qualified professional for your brief.

7

Work Completed

The assigned freelancer executes the agreed deliverables with milestone check-ins.

8

Delivery & Completion

Deliverables are reviewed, verified against requirements, and the project is marked Completed.

The Freelancer Process

How selected professionals unlock and deliver matching opportunities.

1

Receive Invitation

You receive an exclusive invitation code from FidoConnect to access the curated network.

2

Join via Invite Gate

Enter your invite code during registration to create your verified freelancer account.

3

Setup Skill Profile

Select your primary service categories, specific tools, and write a professional introduction.

4

Profile Verification

Operations reviews your profile to ensure quality alignment with incoming client briefs.

5

Select Membership Plan

Choose an active membership plan tier that suits your desired active application volume.

6

Matching Work Unlocked

Live projects matching your verified skill categories are unlocked in the Find Work directory.

7

Submit Application

Review project details and submit your delivery timeframe and proposal within your plan limits.

8

Review & Assignment

FidoConnect reviews candidate applications and assigns the project to the best-matched specialist.

9

Professional Execution

Deliver the work according to agreed specifications, maintaining clear communication.

10

Project Completion

Hand over deliverables. The project is logged in your Completed Projects track record.

Please Note: FidoConnect coordinates project matching based on live client demand and skill fit. Project opportunity availability is not guaranteed, and membership is not a guarantee of continuous employment.
